# Gameplay Update: Custom Status Conditions & Move Changes

Welcome to the newest combat update! We've overhauled the battle system to include 6 custom status conditions that are natively tied to specific Pokémon types. These statuses can swing the tide of battle if utilized correctly.

## New Status Conditions

Here are the 6 new custom statuses and what they do:

*   **Bleeding (Steel):** Deals damage at the end of each round. The damage scales up each turn, functioning exactly like bad poison (Toxic).
*   **Shaken (Ground):** The afflicted Pokémon's physical Defense is halved, and their critical hit stage is reduced by 1, making them easier to defeat and less likely to score critical hits.
*   **Blindness (Psychic):** The afflicted Pokémon has a 20% chance to fail executing any move they attempt to use.
*   **Exploited (Dark):** The afflicted Pokémon's weaknesses are fully exposed. Any Super Effective move that hits them will deal an additional 1.5x damage!
*   **Fear (Dragon):** The afflicted Pokémon is completely paralyzed by fear and cannot select or execute any damaging moves for 3 turns.
*   **Reckless (Fighting):** The afflicted Pokémon is forced into a reckless frenzy for 4 turns. They cannot use status moves, and every time they successfully land an attack, they will take 1/8th of their maximum HP in recoil damage!

## Updated Moves

To accompany these new mechanics, several existing moves have been updated to inflict these statuses:

*   **Cut (HM):** The HM move Cut has been changed from Normal to **Steel-type** for better combat versatility. It now has a 30% chance to leave the target Bleeding.
*   **Psystrike:** Now has a 30% chance to leave the target Blinded.
*   **High Horsepower:** Now has a 30% chance to leave the target Shaken.
*   **False Surrender:** Now has a 30% chance to leave the target Exploited.
*   **Dragon Claw:** Now has a 30% chance to strike Fear into the target.
*   **Vacuum Wave:** Now has a 30% chance to make the target Reckless.

Happy battling!
